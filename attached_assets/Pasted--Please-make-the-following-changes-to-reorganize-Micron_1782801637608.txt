⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

The Process: A licensed provider uses a device (often a motorized, pen-like tool) to repeatedly puncture the top layers of the skin with tiny needles.

The Healing: These micro-injuries stimulate dermal remodeling. As your skin heals, it ramps up the production of structural proteins (collagen and elastin), resulting in firmer, smoother skin.

Product Absorption: The micro-channels created by the needles allow active skincare ingredients (like hyaluronic acid or serums) to penetrate much deeper into the skin.

✨ BENEFITS

Reduces the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles

Diminishes acne scars and surgical scars

Improves enlarged pores and uneven skin tone or texture

Softens stretch marks

⏰ WHAT TO EXPECT & DOWNTIME

The Session: A topical numbing cream is typically applied 30–45 minutes prior to the procedure to ensure comfort. A session usually takes 30 to 60 minutes.

Results: Results are not immediate, as collagen production takes time. Patients typically see optimal results 4 to 6 weeks after their session.

Downtime & Side Effects: Immediately after the procedure, your skin will look and feel like you have a moderate sunburn. Redness, swelling, and mild sensitivity generally resolve within 24 to 48 hours.

💡 IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS
